vamp n. A short figure the band repeats so soloists can come in and go out.

The protocol is the vamp: a steady figure of assets, revisions and events that apps and agents play over.

AVAMP is an open media-catalog protocol: an MIT-licensed contract, Rust types and generated JSON Schemas. Wire 0.1, experimental.

BReference-only. Originals stay where they are, identified by the BLAKE3 of their exact bytes.

01 The figure

Four beats,
on repeat.

Under every client the same four moves repeat for every file. Learn them once and you can come in on any bar: an app, a script, an agent.

1

1. Asset

A stable UUID, independent of the file’s path. The original is referenced in place and identified by the BLAKE3 of its exact bytes.

"id": "bc8b501d-…-5c1d5c296698",
"original": {
  "blake3": "504de3d9…6d22dfc0",
  "path": "/tmp/refs/moon-bat.jpg",
  "storage": "local"
}

2

2. Revision

Every metadata change advances the revision. Send --expected-revision and a stale write is rejected without changing metadata or emitting an event.

{"error": {
  "code": "operation_failed",
  "message": "revision conflict: expected 1, current 2"
}, "protocol_version": "0.1"}

3

3. Event

Every change is an ordered event with one monotonic sequence per library. One local authority writes; replicas keep their last sequence and ask for what came after.

{"sequence": 2,
 "kind": "media_updated",
 "actor": "agent:lander-human-machine",
 "revision": 2,
 "payload": {"change": "metadata"}}

4

4. Observation

Processors are registered local executables, run with argv and never a shell. One ToolRequest in on stdin, one ToolResponse out: captions, tags, embeddings. Originals are never touched.

"required": ["name", "events",
  "command", "input", "output"],
"triggers": ["media_added",
  "media_updated",
  "representation_ready"]

      04 Agents
      
      
      Trading
       fours.
      
      
      Agents work in the library with the same CLI, and vamp guide prints their manual. A research mission is an exploration: a folder of findings, each filed with its source. The owner answers in the app with ratings and tags; the agent reads them back on its next turn.
