FAQ

What happens when the trial ends?
Vampire switches to viewer mode. Browsing, search, the viewer, tags and folders, export, sync and connecting to a server keep working. Importing, clipping, screenshots and Eagle import ask for a license. Nothing is deleted or locked.
Can I always get my data out?
Yes. Your originals stay on your disks as they are, and the MIT vamp CLI reads and writes the same library with or without Vampire.
How do I activate it?
We email your license key with an Activate button. Click it on your Mac, or paste the key into Vampire, Settings, License.
I lost my key.
Use Find my license with the email you bought with. We send you a link to your account, where you can see your key and manage your Macs.
I have a 4th Mac.
Activate it anyway and Vampire asks which Mac to deactivate, or deactivate one from your account. Macs not seen for 180 days stop counting.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Vampire checks its license about once a day when it can. Without a connection it keeps working fully for about four months, then drops to viewer mode until it can check again.
What does "every future update" mean?
Every new version of Vampire for Mac, major versions included.
How do refunds work?
Email support@vamp.sh within 30 days of paying and we refund you.
Do I need to pay to share my library?
No. Use vamp serve on your own machine and vamp join on a replica to connect the people, machines and agents you admit. This is whole-library access, not hosted public links.
